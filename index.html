      Press Given to stamp the time (editable). Every step marked Given is printed in the referral letter. Never delay transfer for labs. MgSO₄ stock: 25% (250 mg/mL), 20 mL bottle = 5 g.

      Before each MgSO₄ maintenance hour: patellar reflex present, RR above 12/min, urine ≥100 mL in the last 4 h. If any is not met, withhold and reassess. Toxicity: stop MgSO₄; calcium gluconate 10% 10 mL IV over 2–5 min; support breathing.
